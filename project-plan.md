## 2. Project Scope and Learning Goals

**Target Learners:** 
First-year university students with basic user-level computer skills, but no background in computer hardware, networking basics, or behaviorist AI concepts.

**Project Purpose:** 
To develop a beginner-friendly educational material consisting of a Study Guide, PPT Slides, and Video Tutorials that introduce foundational concepts. This material aims to help learners understand the mechanics behind the technology they use every day by translating complex concepts into easy analogies and step-by-step explanations.

**Learning Goals:**
By the end of this module, learners should be able to:
1. Identify the core physical components of computers and explain their basic functions.
2. Describe how data travels between connected devices using networks.
3. Explain the basic principles of how AI models use data to generate predictions.
4. Understand how hardware, networks, and AI integrate in everyday devices.
5. Recognize the limitations of AI and the importance of security and human oversight.

**Content Outline:**
1.  **Computer Hardware Basics:** CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, sensors, I/O devices.
    - *Status:* Waiting for review.
2.  **Network Basics:** Networks, routers, servers, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, data packets, latency.
    - *Status:* Waiting for review.
3.  **Artificial Intelligence Basics:** AI models, training data, inference, predictions.
    - *Status:* TBD.
4.  **How Hardware, Networks, and AI Work Together:** Data processing, edge AI, cloud AI.
    - *Status:* TBD.
5.  **AI Behaviour and Limitations:** Model outputs, bias, incomplete data, human oversight.
    - *Status:* TBD.
6.  **Real-World Applications of AI:** Smart homes, robotics, autonomous vehicles.
    - *Status:* TBD.
7.  **Security, Privacy, and Reliability:** Data privacy, cybersecurity, system failures.
    - *Status:* TBD.

**Scope Boundaries:** 
We will deeply explain the foundational concepts in Topics 1, 2, and 3 using everyday analogies. For Topics 4–7, we will provide a high-level overview of how these technologies integrate and affect society, avoiding deep technical math or complex protocols to keep the material accessible to absolute beginners.

## 3. Planned Learning Resources

- **Shared Markdown Content:** All explanations, terminology, and examples will be drafted in the `/content/` folder as Markdown files. This serves as our single source of truth.
- **Study Guide & Test Bank:** The Study Guide will adapt the Markdown into a textbook format, including chapter summaries and a quiz with explained answers at the end of each section.
- **PPT Slides:** Slides will use visual diagrams (e.g., flowcharts) and short bullet points to support the live lecture.
- **Video Tutorials:** Videos will explain the topic and focus on complex concepts or processes that are hard to visualize.

## 4. Workflow and Coordination

**Workflow & GitHub Usage:**
We use VS Code and GitHub for tracking changes. `main` can be edited directly only for meeting summaries and `tasks.md` updates. Members create task branches (e.g., `content-topic-1`), commit their work, and open Pull Requests (PRs). A teammate (Quality Lead) reviews the PR before it is merged.

**Communication & Review:**
We communicate daily via a WeChat group and hold in-person meetings at the 24HR Study Point. Peer review is conducted entirely through GitHub PRs, ensuring all corrections are documented.

**Schedule & Internal Deadlines:**
To ensure high quality, all internal deadlines are set 1 day before the official Lexue deadlines.
- **Stage 1 (Due Oct 24):** Planning, content foundation, and first developed topic. *(Internal deadline: Oct 23)*
- **Stage 2 (Due Nov 7):** All content drafted, first versions of slides/videos ready. *(Internal deadline: Nov 6)*
- **Stage 3 (Due Nov 28):** Final review, proofreading, and final delivery. *(Internal deadline: Nov 27)*

**Main Risks and Responses:**
1.  *Risk:* Team members unfamiliar with Git/VS Code.
    - *Response:* The CEO created a `manual.md` and hosted a live setup meeting. 
2.  *Risk:* Uneven workload.
    - *Response:* Cross-role helpers (e.g., CIO helps Content Lead) ensure no one is overloaded. All tasks are tracked in `tasks.md`.
