# Task List

> ## Rules
> **Status Key:**
> - **To Do:** Not started yet.
> - **In Progress:** Currently being worked on.
> - **In Review:** Pull Request opened, waiting for Quality Lead / Teammate to approve.
> - **Done:** Approved and merged into `main`.
> 
> **Logging & Completion Rules:**
> 1. **Status Authority:** Only the Quality Lead and CEO change a status to `Done`.
> 2. **Project Log:** Whenever a task is marked `Done` (merged into `main`), the Owner **must immediately** add an entry to `/logs/project-log.md` with a screenshot of the PR.
> 3. **Personal Logs:** Personal logs are tracked separately in private Word documents. They do NOT go in GitHub and do NOT count as Project Log entries.
