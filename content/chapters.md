# Beginner Learning Topics

## Topic 1: Computer Hardware Basics
- **Research Focus:** The main physical components of computers and their functions.
- **Key Concepts:** CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, sensors, input devices, and output devices.
- **Learning Goal:** Understand how hardware receives, processes, and stores information.
- **Example:** How a smart camera captures an image and processes it.

## Topic 2: Network Basics
- **Research Focus:** How computers and other devices communicate and exchange information.
- **Key Concepts:** Networks, routers, servers, Wi-Fi, Ethernet, data packets, bandwidth, and latency.
- **Learning Goal:** Understand how data travels between connected devices.
- **Example:** How a phone receives information from an online server.

## Topic 3: Artificial Intelligence Basics
- **Research Focus:** The basic principles of AI and how AI models produce results.
- **Key Concepts:** AI models, training data, inference, predictions, inputs, and outputs.
- **Learning Goal:** Understand how an AI model uses information to generate a result.
- **Example:** How an AI model identifies objects in a photograph.

## Topic 4: How Hardware, Networks, and AI Work Together
- **Research Focus:** The relationship between hardware, network communication, and AI processing.
- **Key Concepts:** Data processing, edge AI, cloud AI, processing time, and network latency.
- **Learning Goal:** Understand how different technologies work together to complete a task.
- **Example:** How a smart doorbell detects a visitor and sends a notification to a phone.

## Topic 5: AI Behaviour and Limitations
- **Research Focus:** How AI systems behave and why their results are not always correct.
- **Key Concepts:** Model outputs, confidence scores, bias, incomplete data, evaluation, and human oversight.
- **Learning Goal:** Recognise the limitations of AI and understand why its results need to be evaluated.
- **Example:** Why an AI recognition system may perform differently under different lighting conditions.

## Topic 6: Real-World Applications of AI
- **Research Focus:** How AI is used in everyday devices and different industries.
- **Key Concepts:** Smart homes, smartphones, robotics, autonomous vehicles, healthcare applications, and recommendation systems.
- **Learning Goal:** Identify practical uses of AI and the technologies that support them.
- **Example:** How a smart home camera detects movement and identifies a person.

## Topic 7: Security, Privacy, and Reliability
- **Research Focus:** The risks and responsibilities involved in using connected devices and AI systems.
- **Key Concepts:** Data privacy, cybersecurity, data protection, reliability, system failures, and human oversight.
- **Learning Goal:** Understand how to protect information and make AI systems safer and more dependable.
- **Example:** How a smart doorbell protects recorded images and handles an internet connection failure.

## Research and Development Plan

For each topic, the team should aim to include:

- A beginner-friendly explanation of the main concepts.
- Definitions of important technical terms.
- A labelled diagram or visual explanation.
- A real-world example.
- A worked example or practical demonstration, where appropriate.
- A short activity or quiz with an explained answer.
- Reliable references for further learning.

## Suggested Learning Order

1. Computer Hardware Basics
2. Network Basics
3. Artificial Intelligence Basics
4. How Hardware, Networks, and AI Work Together
5. AI Behaviour and Limitations
6. Real-World Applications of AI
7. Security, Privacy, and Reliability
